Turning a Trust-Concerned Inbound Prospect into a Confident Paying Client

Role
Sales Development Representative
Challenge
Trust & Payment Hesitation
Result
Conversion Secured

Situation: A prospect from Ghana expressed strong interest but hesitated due to past negative experiences with online payments. She requested physical office or deferred payment options, risking conversion loss.

What I Did: I recognized this was a trust issue, not a pricing issue. Instead of pushing for payment, I focused on reducing perceived risk:

  • → Responded via email with reassurance, not pressure
  • → Offered live verification options (Google Meet platform walkthrough)
  • → Provided clear breakdown of deliverables post-payment
  • → Shared structured onboarding process to demonstrate legitimacy
  • → Used HubSpot CRM to track engagement stage and schedule follow-ups
  • → Moved conversation to Aircall for deeper engagement

Tools Used: HubSpot, Email, Google Meet, Aircall

Skills Applied: Empathy, Objection Handling, Persuasive Communication, Emotional Intelligence

Result: Client moved from hesitation to confidence → Agreed to structured payment plan → Successfully onboarded
